A cozy mystery puzzle game

Someone in the mansion is a killer. Put everyone in their place.

Innocent or guilty? In Sudokiller, your mission is to reconstruct the night of the crime by placing every suspect exactly where they were. Read the clues, follow the logic, and uncover the one arrangement that reveals who the killer was.

Every case takes place in a new room filled with charming animal suspects, suspicious circumstances, and one very inconvenient murder. The solution is already there. You just have to deduce it.

Gameplay

Read the clues.
Place the suspects.
Crack the case.

Each case gives you a crime scene, a cast of suspects, and a series of clues describing where everyone was when the murder happened. Your job is to place every suspect on a sudoku-style grid until the entire scene fits together.

A clue might tell you who was sitting down, who was alone, who was inside a certain room, who stood next to a bookshelf, or which two suspects were in the same area. Every detail narrows the possibilities.

As you rule out impossible positions, the mystery starts to solve itself. The victim takes the final open spot, the scene is complete, and the arrangement reveals the most important answer of all: who committed the murder.

Progress through new rooms and mansions as cases grow from approachable introductions into increasingly challenging deductions, always at your own pace.

1

Study the crime scene

Look at the rooms, furniture, objects, and spaces that make each position on the grid unique.

2

Follow every clue

Use relationships, locations, occupied objects, and other details to eliminate impossible placements.

3

Place every suspect

Build the only arrangement that satisfies all the clues. No random guesses are required.

4

Unmask the killer

Complete the scene and discover who was with the victim when the crime took place.

Features

Cozy crime scenes.
Serious deduction.

Sudokiller combines the satisfaction of a logic puzzle with the charm of a cozy mystery. Take your time, read every clue, and let deduction do the detective work.

Puzzle

Every case is a self-contained logic puzzle with a single arrangement waiting to be uncovered.

Pure Logic

Follow the clues and eliminate possibilities. No guessing or trial and error is needed to solve a case.

Single Player

Investigate at your own pace with a puzzle experience designed for focused, relaxing solo play.

Cozy Mystery

Murder is the mystery, not the mood: charming rooms, cute suspects, and low-pressure puzzle solving keep every investigation cozy.

Play Detective

Reconstruct each crime scene by working out where every suspect was at the exact moment of the murder.

Hand-drawn

Explore a colorful 2D world filled with hand-drawn rooms, objects, characters, and little visual details.

Growing Challenge

Start with approachable investigations and work your way toward more complex cases as new rooms and mansions unlock.

Rooms Full of Clues

Bedrooms, kitchens, greenhouses, attics, and more turn each case into a new spatial puzzle to understand.
