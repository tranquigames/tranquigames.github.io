Sudokiller

The Game

Someone in the mansion is a killer.

Innocent or guilty? In Sudokiller, your mission is to reconstruct the night of the crime by placing every suspect exactly where they were. Read the clues, follow the logic, and uncover the one arrangement that reveals who the killer was.

Every case gives you a new crime scene, a group of suspects, and a trail of clues. The solution is already there. You just have to deduce it.

Gameplay

Read the clues. Place the suspects. Crack the case.

Each case gives you a crime scene, a cast of suspects, and a series of clues describing where everyone was when the murder happened. Your job is to place every suspect on a sudoku-style grid until the entire scene fits together.

Who was sitting down? Who was alone? Who was in the kitchen? Who was next to a bookshelf? Every detail narrows down the possibilities.

As you rule out impossible positions, the scene slowly comes together. Complete the arrangement and reveal what happened and, most importantly, who the killer was.

1

Study the scene

Understand the rooms, furniture, objects, and spaces around the grid.

2

Follow the clues

Use locations and relationships to eliminate impossible positions.

3

Place every suspect

Find the arrangement that satisfies every clue without guessing.

4

Unmask the killer

Finish the reconstruction and discover who committed the murder.
