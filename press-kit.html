Sudokiller

Innocent or guilty? In Sudokiller, your mission is to place every suspect exactly where they were the night of the crime. It's a cozy puzzle mystery game where you're in charge of cracking the case, one clue at a time.

Whether it's a guest bedroom, a busy kitchen, a cluttered greenhouse, or a hidden attic, every crime scene introduces a fresh cast of suspects with their own alibis and habits. A single missing piece of information can turn an open-and-shut case into a full-blown investigation. It's all about reading the clues to find the one arrangement that fits!

Each case unfolds inside a different room of a house full of secrets, filled with charming animal suspects and a victim who always takes the last open seat. Piece together who was where using pure deduction, no guessing, no trial and error, just one logical solution waiting to be uncovered.

  • Play detective and place every suspect using sudoku-style logic.
  • Who sat where, who had company, and who was never really alone.
  • Piece together satisfying puzzles with cozy scenarios to explore and cases to crack at your own pace.
  • Unlock new rooms and mansions as you progress, from easy to expert cases.

Design Assets

Two People Making Games with Care

Based in Buenos Aires, Tranqui Games was founded by Rev and Manu, a two-person team brought together by a shared love of videogames and the desire to create something of their own. Rev brings experience in content creation and marketing while taking her first steps into game development, and Manu contributes his previous experience as a game designer.

Sudokiller is their first game developed together as a team. It is a cozy deduction puzzle game where players must reconstruct crime scenes by following clues, placing each suspect on a grid-based map, and using pure logic to uncover who committed the crime.

Sudokiller reflects Tranqui Games' goal of creating playful, approachable games that are satisfying to understand and memorable to experience. The studio hopes to explore original ideas with personality, combining thoughtful mechanics, charming visual worlds, and, above all, games that people genuinely enjoy playing.
